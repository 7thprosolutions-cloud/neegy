ARENA

One arena. One rival. Build cover, take the high ground, win the fight.

A / D or ← / → — move
W or ↑ — jump
S or ↓ — duck
Mouse / F — shoot (hold)
Q — build wall
E — build ramp

Walls block shots and can be destroyed. Ramps let you climb for high ground. Both cost a short cooldown and you can only have a few up at once.
